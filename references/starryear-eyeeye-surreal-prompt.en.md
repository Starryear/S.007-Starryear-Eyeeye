# Starryear-EyeEye | Original Behind, Uncanny World Centered

Treat one user-supplied photograph as immutable photographic evidence and as the sole source of content, composition, and color for the generated scene. Return one flattened vertical artwork: the crop-only original fills the entire rear layer, while a smaller centered portrait rectangle translates the same subjects into hand-painted beings with two minimal eyes. Use recursion, scale collapse, or impossible interior space to make the inset cute, absurd, quiet, and distinctly uncanny.

## 1. Fixed layout

- Default to a vertical 2:3 canvas unless the user specifies another ratio. Return one final image only.
- Fill the canvas with a proportionally scaled and cropped copy of the photograph. Do not repaint, filter, retouch, generatively extend, replace content, add eyes, or stylize this rear layer.
- Center a portrait inset occupying about 66–76% of canvas width and 68–80% of canvas height. A clearly visible photographic band must remain on all four sides.
- Use a clean rectangular crop edge with no white mat, stroke, shadow, torn paper, tape, caption, or decorative frame.
- Keep the rear layer unmistakably photographic and the inset unmistakably hand-painted. Do not apply one style across the whole canvas.

## 2. Source observation and reconstruction

Internally identify the source's decisive subjects, silhouettes, clustering, occlusion, material marks, light-dark path, dominant and accent colors, negative space, and movement. Every major inset body, color, light cue, and spatial rhythm must derive from this evidence. Consolidate minor noise into legible large, medium, and small forms; invent no unrelated character, species, object, building, or location.

Preserve prototype-specific irregularity. Pumpkins retain ribs, stems, scuffs, squat variation, and pile structure; foliage retains branching, feathering, and trailing direction; stones retain fractures and weight; fabric retains folds and tension. Do not force all subjects into smooth balls or capsules.

## 3. One dominant absurd spatial idea

Choose one primary mechanism:

1. Source subjects repeat from large to small into a recursive tunnel.
2. Outer subjects form an impossible interior containing more of themselves.
3. Scale collapses progressively toward the center, suggesting endless recession.
4. A source cluster becomes a self-containing loop, collective gaze, or endless procession.
5. Folded depth makes the same subject appear both container and contained.

Allow one secondary mutation only when it strengthens the same proposition. Do not stack generic portals, outer space, floating islands, tentacles, or giant eyes. The uncanny quality must arise from recursion, crowd behavior, scale failure, impossible containment, and coordinated gaze—not from monsters.

Anchor the inset perimeter with large source-derived beings, lead inward with medium beings, and deepen the recursion with small beings. Place the strongest impossible depth near the optical center or lower-middle and keep it fully visible. Avoid uniform rows, random confetti, identical clones, and directionless crowding.

## 4. Relationship between rear photo and inset

- Match the same subject family, palette, light direction, and broad mass distribution so the inset feels like an alternate interior opened inside the photograph.
- Do not require pixel continuity at the rectangle edge and do not fake photographic seamlessness. Build the bridge through recognition, placement, color echo, light, and contour rhyme.
- Make the inset the first visual read while preserving enough rear-photo border to keep its origin legible.

## 5. EyeEye beings

- Enforce: one body = one being = one pair of eyes.
- Every independent body—including distant, pale, translucent, occluded, or edge beings—has exactly two small dark pigment marks.
- No continuous body with multiple pairs, floating eyes, eyeless independent forms, or mechanical eye stickers on an undivided surface.
- Vary dots, flattened ovals, very short dashes, subtle high-low pairs, and lightly veiled marks within roughly 70–130% of the group average.
- Direct many gazes toward the recursive center or nearby beings to create quiet collective awareness, without making every pair identical.
- Add no mouth, nose, brows, sclera, realistic pupil construction, lashes, sockets, or complete face. Exclude giant eyeballs and frightening facial anatomy.

## 6. Material, color, and light

- Render the inset as tactile watercolor and gouache on paper: short visible strokes, dry-brush breaks, translucent layers, granular sediment, matte tooth, soft bloom, and selected thicker pigment.
- Keep silhouettes readable despite soft edges. Exclude plastic toys, glossy 3D, vector outlines, heavy glow, photographic cutouts, and full-image filters.
- Derive the entire palette from the photograph's dominant hues, highlights, shadows, and accents. An optional style reference may influence only abstract treatment, never its subjects, exact composition, palette, text, logo, signature, or watermark.
- Let the recursive center become darker and denser than the perimeter, but preserve readable bodies, texture, and chromatic depth rather than turning it into a featureless black hole.

## 7. Emotional boundary

Aim for “cute at first glance, absurd on the second, faintly chilling when watched longer.” Let spatial logic and collective gaze create unease. Exclude blood, rot, wounds, dismemberment, fangs, attack, screaming, predation, gore, or shock imagery.

Add no unrelated people, hands, realistic animals, buildings, props, text, numbers, dates, titles, logos, signatures, watermarks, frames, or panel dividers. If such content already exists in the background photograph, preserve it unchanged there; in the inset, translate only its visual mass into a non-realistic form without reproducing identity or a realistic face.

## 8. Final audit

Confirm that the photograph remains realistic, unaltered, and full-bleed behind the inset; the centered rectangle leaves the photograph visible on all sides and has no frame or shadow; the inset unmistakably derives from the same source; one dominant recursive or impossible-space idea is clear and uncropped; every independent being has exactly one minimal pair of eyes; the tone is cute, absurd, and uncanny without horror; and no text, logo, signature, watermark, or extra panel appears.

Return only one flattened final image. Do not return the source separately, isolated layer files, alternatives, comparison sheets, analysis, or prompt notes.
